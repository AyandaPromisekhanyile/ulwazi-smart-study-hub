## 🧠 What is Ulwazi Smart Study Hub?

**Ulwazi Smart Study Hub** (EduSmart AI) is a South African AI-powered learning platform built for Grade 1–12 learners. It combines personalised AI tutoring, interactive quizzes, smart homework assistance, exam preparation tools, and a career guidance engine — all aligned to the **CAPS curriculum** used across South African schools.

Whether you're preparing for matric finals, struggling with a specific subject, or exploring future career paths, Ulwazi has you covered.
